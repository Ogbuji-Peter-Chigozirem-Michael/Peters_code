The city

City is a large, permanent human settlement with a high density of population and infrastructure. Unlike small towns or villages, cities feature complex social structures, specialized non-agricultural jobs, and advanced systems for housing, transportation, and public utilities.
  • High Density: Many people live and work close together in a limited space.
  • Infrastructure: Advanced systems for water, waste, electricity, and roads.
  • Local Government: An official legal status or charter granted by the state or government.
